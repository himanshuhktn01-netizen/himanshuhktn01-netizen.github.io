THE GAME

GAME OVERVIEW

Goblin Slayer is a 2D action-survival shooter developed using Python and Pygame. The player must defend a village against successive waves of Goblins and powerful Bosses. Survival depends on shooting enemies, managing ammunition, maintaining health and using available resources carefully.

YOUR MISSION

DEFEND THE VILLAGE

Survive all incoming enemy waves and defeat the enemies attacking the village.

VICTORY CONDITION

Complete all enemy waves to win the game.

DEFEAT CONDITION

The game ends when the player's health reaches zero.

SURVIVE EACH ENCOUNTER

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Every system supports the fight to protect the village.

ENEMY SYSTEM

Fight Goblins and powerful Bosses as they attack the village.

SHOOTING SYSTEM

Shoot incoming enemies to defend the village.

AMMUNITION

Monitor your ammunition and reload when necessary.

HEALTH

Maintain the player's health and use the health feature.

WALLET

WAVE MANAGEMENT

Progress through successive enemy waves.
